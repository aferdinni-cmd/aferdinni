The course

How do I get started?

From finding an instructor to the full licence – the whole process in 12 steps.

  1. 1

    Find a driving instructor

    Choose your driving instructor and get in touch.

  2. 2

    Apply for a learner's permit

    Apply for a learner's permit online at Ísland.is. You upload a passport photo if one is not already in the system, along with the student's signature at the District Commissioner.

  3. 3

    Driving school 1 and practical lessons

    At least 10 practical driving lessons.

  4. 4

    Practice driving

    Guidance and conditions for accompanying drivers are available on the Icelandic Transport Authority website.

  5. 5

    Driving school 2

    Driving school 2 is valid for 2 years and after that it must be repeated.

  6. 6

    Driving school 3

    Before taking driving school 3 you must complete driving school 1 and at least 10 driving lessons.

  7. 7

    Written test

    To register for the written test, a student must have completed the driving school courses and 12 practical lessons.

  8. 8

    Practical lessons

    Preparation for the practical test. To take the practical test a student must have passed the written driving test and completed at least 15 practical lessons.

  9. 9

    Practical test

    Here the student answers questions from the examiner (oral test) and passes the practical part of the driving test.

  10. 10

    Driving licence – temporary licence

    If the student passes the practical test, they receive a temporary driving licence valid for 3 years.

  11. 11

    Driving assessment

    The student attends a driving assessment with an instructor, which can be done after the first year with a temporary licence. This is the final step to the full licence.

  12. 12

    Full licence

    The full licence is issued after the driving assessment with an instructor, provided the student has no active penalty points on their temporary licence.

How lessons are arranged

  • Lessons follow a lesson plan.
  • Lessons are booked in advance and entered in Noona.
  • Cancellations must be reported as early as possible.
  • Please arrive on time.

Good cooperation is the key to success

  • The instructor provides professional teaching, guidance and assessment of the student's skills.
  • The student takes an active part and is responsible for their own progress.
  • Parents and guardians support the learning and create good conditions for practice driving.

Student responsibilities

Attendance and punctuality
Arrive on time for driving lessons and other booked appointments.
Preparation
Familiarise yourself with the material and read what has been covered – on the study site on this website.
Responsibility
Take an active part in the learning and follow the instructor's directions.
Honesty
Be open about what is going well and what needs more practice.
Practice
Use the opportunity for practice driving with an accompanying driver when relevant.
Safety
Show responsibility, consideration and respect in traffic.
Attitude
Be ready to take guidance, learn from mistakes and improve your skills.

Guardian responsibilities

Support and encouragement
Show a positive attitude and encourage the student onwards.
Practice driving
Give the student regular opportunities to practise with an approved accompanying driver. Information and good advice are available on the Icelandic Transport Authority website.
Patience
Show calm and understanding while the student is learning something new.
Cooperation
Stay in good contact with the instructor and follow their advice.
Safety
Make sure practice driving follows current rules and is done safely.
Positive approach
Avoid unnecessary criticism and focus on constructive feedback.
Role model
Set a good example in traffic through consideration and responsible behaviour.
